PROJECT PROCESS

How the analysis was completed

Four defined stages, from the original business questions to decision-ready recommendations.

01

Business questions

Where is patient demand concentrated? Which disease and admission patterns affect capacity? How do payment methods and regions contribute to revenue?

02

Data preparation

Profiled and standardised the simulated dataset, checked missing and duplicate records, reviewed field consistency and shaped analysis-ready categories.

03

Analysis and model

Structured measures for patients, admissions and revenue; segmented results by department, specialisation, payment type and state; designed drillable KPI views.

04

Recommendations

Highlighted demand in Cardiology and General Medicine, frequent UPI and insurance use, seasonal admission pressure and regional revenue concentration for staffing and planning review.

Portfolio case study based on a public reference brief and simulated healthcare data. It is presented as analytical practice, not client work.
